Crazy 8's Card Game
Project Overview

This project is a Crazy 8’s card game built in SwiftUI. The game is inspired by Kyle’s Crazy 8’s and follows the traditional gameplay style while adding extra features and improvements.

The player starts the game from a main menu and competes against computer-controlled players. The objective is to get rid of all cards before the other players. Players take turns matching cards by suit or value, and special rules apply when playing an 8 card.

The game includes a deck of cards, a discard pile, player hands, computer opponents, save functionality, and a history of recently played cards.

Game Rules:
The game uses a standard deck of cards.
Players take turns placing cards that match either the suit or value of the top card on the discard pile.
If a player cannot play a card, they must draw from the deck.
Playing an 8 card allows the player to choose a new suit.
The first player to get rid of all cards wins the game.
Computer players automatically choose and play valid cards during their turns.

Gameplay Features

~Draw card button when no playable cards are available.

~Card selection system that allows players to choose cards from their hand.

~Pass button when no valid move exists.

~Visual display of the most recently played card.

~Discard pile and deck displayed on screen.

~Turn-based gameplay with computer opponents.

Computer Players
~AI opponents can only see the back of other players’ cards.

~Computer players automatically choose valid cards during their turn.
